# SIH26045 demo path

1. Open http://127.0.0.1:8502 and start a new chat. Select India and English.
2. Ask `What is Section 3(p)?` to demonstrate a cited answer. Open its Evidence passport to inspect the actual source page and download provenance.
3. Ask `I want to export my herbal product abroad`. The assistant should ask for the destination, not guess one. Reply `Singapore`. It must retain that detail and either provide sourced guidance or state the coverage limitation.
4. Open Product brief & review tools. Save only a product's intended use and claim. Click Check classification. Missing route/composition facts should be requested. The form must not award a category or imply approval.
5. Complete a fictional, non-confidential brief and request Review ABS. Inspect citations and any requested missing facts; do not present its output as regulatory clearance.
6. Switch to International and ask `How does the Budapest Treaty help with microorganism deposits for patents?`. Confirm international citations stay separate from India.
7. Switch the answer language to Tamil or Hindi. Test an individual answer's language selector and confirm its reference numbers stay unchanged.
8. Open Source freshness: demonstrate acquisition date, local file state and version notes. Explain that retrieval date does not prove legal currency.
9. Download Expert review pack. Check it contains the original question/answer, jurisdiction, profile and cited evidence. Use the WIPO TISC link to locate a human facilitator. No message is sent.
10. Check Privacy & audit and download the content-free audit. Delete the demo conversation using its existing delete control; remove any test upload separately.

Use fabricated product facts in demonstrations. A citation or passing software test is not proof that the generated legal analysis is correct. Full corpus updates, expert legal review, voice, relational legal knowledge graph, paid connectors and multi-user production hardening are outside the completed local MVP.
