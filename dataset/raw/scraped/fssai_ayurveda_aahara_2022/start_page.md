## India's Food Safety Authority Ensuring
## Safe And Healthy Food
The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) is responsible for regulating and supervising food safety across the country. It sets science-based standards, enables compliance for food businesses, and ensures the availability of safe and wholesome food for consumers.
